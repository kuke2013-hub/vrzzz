Quest controls · v11.1
LEFT stick LEFT/RIGHT: move Zelda left or right (not the viewer), whether or not you grip her.
EITHER stick BACK/FORWARD: pull Zelda toward you or push her away, with or without gripping.
Two side grips: spread hands to enlarge, bring them together to shrink. Linear scaling: 10%–350% of original size, with natural hand-spacing control.
RIGHT wrist during two-hand grab: pitch Zelda up/down and roll left/right while scaling.
RIGHT stick LEFT/RIGHT while gripping: rotate Zelda 30° per tilt. Without a right grip, turn your viewpoint 30°.
Release grip: Zelda stays fixed without gravity. Press Reset to restore normal size and position.

Desktop: Drag to orbit · Scroll to zoom · WASD to move
